# Welcome to the Hindley Multiscale Biodesign Lab

We are a research group aiming to engineer new biological systems across length scales, from the bottom-up. In doing so, our work connects the study and engineering of interacting molecular networks (nanoscale), the creation of synthetic cells (microscale), and studying the interactions of synthetic cells with living systems and materials (mesoscale).
